Feeding wildlife

Why is bread bad for the lake?
Rotting bread sinks and decaying food lowers oxygen levels in the water
Harm to eels - Low oxygen can make it hard for eels to survive
💡 Tip: Bring healthy seeds instead of bread

Risks to wildlife
Plastic hazards - Litter can trap or injure animals

Nesting season

Why nests matter
Disturbing a nest can cause parent birds to abandon their eggs
Keep dogs on leads and stick to paths near nesting areas
💡 Tip: Watch nests from a distance with binoculars

Water quality

Why clean water matters
Chemicals and detergents harm fish, insects and plants
Never rinse paint, oil or cleaning products near the lake
💡 Tip: Report any spills to a park ranger straight away
